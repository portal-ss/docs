# Referral program

Invite people you know to KYC Portal and earn bonuses on their purchases.

## What you get

- **10%** of every verification your referral buys at the standard price, once the verification has passed.
- **10%** of every subscription payment they make, including renewals.

Bonuses are credited to your referral balance automatically. You don't earn a bonus on your referral's wholesale or team orders, re-verifications or balance top-ups.

## How to invite

Every client has a personal referral link. You can find yours:

- in the shop bot [@kyctrade_bot](https://t.me/kyctrade_bot) — "👤 Profile";
- in the workspace bot [@kycportal_bot](https://t.me/kycportal_bot) — "🎟️ Referrals"; the "Invite" button lets you send an invitation to any chat straight away;
- in the web app — the "Affiliate" section.

The person you invite opens the link and starts the bot, and they're linked to you automatically and permanently.

!!! note
    Linking only works for new users, when they start the bot for the first time. If someone has already used the bots before, the link won't connect them to you.

### Custom link

In the workspace bot ("✏️ Change personal link") and in the web app, you can replace the code in your link with a memorable one of your own. The code must be unique and consist of Latin letters, digits and underscores, with at least one letter. Once you change the code, your old links stop working — so if you've already sent out invitations, it's best not to change it unless you have to.

## Where to see your earnings

- **Shop bot** — "👤 Profile": your referral balance, the number of people you've invited and your link.
- **Workspace bot and web app** — detailed statistics: how much you've earned in total and separately from verifications and subscriptions, the number of people you've invited and your recent earnings.

You can turn off notifications about new earnings in the workspace bot ("Referral reward pushes") or in the web app settings.

## How to use your referral balance {#withdraw}

You can use your referral balance to pay for any of the platform's services; when you pay, it's spent first, before your main balance (see [Balance and top-ups](balance.md)).

Withdrawing the referral balance is available only to platform partners — on request, via "💸 Withdraw referral balance" or through support at [@freidbase](https://t.me/freidbase).
