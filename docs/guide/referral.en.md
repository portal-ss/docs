# Referral program

Your referral balance is credited with:

- **up to 10%** of the price of every verification your referral buys at the standard price, once it passes;
- **10%** of every subscription payment they make, including renewals.

There's no bonus on wholesale and team orders, re-verifications or top-ups.

## Your link

Your link is in "👤 Profile" in the shop bot, in "🎟️ Referrals" in the workspace bot and in "Affiliate" in the web app. The person you invite is linked to you permanently if they start the bot through the link for the first time. Someone who has already used the bots won't be linked.

You can replace the code in the link: "✏️ Change personal link" in the bot or "Change code" in the web app. The code is 5–30 Latin letters, digits or `_`. Old links stop working; your previous code is held for you for 90 days, and you can take it back.

## Earnings

The list of earnings is in "Affiliate". To turn off bonus notifications, use the "Referral reward pushes: Yes" button in "🎟️ Referrals" or the "Bonus notifications" switch in "Affiliate"; it's one shared setting.

## Referral balance {#withdraw}

You can use it to pay for any of the platform's services. Only platform partners can withdraw it: "💸 Withdraw referral balance" ("👤 Profile" in the shop bot or "💵 Balance (…$)" in the workspace bot) or [@freidbase](https://t.me/freidbase).
