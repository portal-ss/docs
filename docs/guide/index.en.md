# Documentation

A guide for KYC Portal clients — people who order account verifications on crypto exchanges. It explains how the platform works, how to pay for and place an order, how to hand over your accounts, how to track progress and what happens to your money when something is cancelled.

If you're new here, start with the [platform overview](overview.md), then go through the [quick start](getting-started.md). The service rules are in the [Terms of Use](../terms.md), and short answers are in the [FAQ](../faq.md). If you can't find what you need, contact support at [@freidbase](https://t.me/freidbase).
