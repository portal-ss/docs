# Subscription

A subscription is paid access for a fixed period that unlocks wholesale prices and team orders.

## What a subscription gives you

- **Wholesale prices** — on the same catalog verifications, lower than the standard prices.
- **Team orders** — orders for [your team](team.md): any country and your own price per slot, all of which goes to the executor.

There's no limit on the number of orders: while your subscription is active, you can create as many wholesale and team orders as you like. Your subscription also affects the price of [re-verifications](reverify.md) for accounts from wholesale and team orders.

## Plans

| Plan | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | 30 days | $30 |
| Pro | 90 days | $75 |

The plans differ only in duration — the access is the same.

## How to buy or renew

- **Workspace bot** — "💳 Subscription" → "📋 Plans". If your subscription is active, the button is called "➕ Extend subscription"; if it has expired and you have frozen slots, it's "➕ Renew and unfreeze". Choose a plan, check the confirmation card (plan, duration, price, balance, end date) and confirm.
- **Web app** — the "Subscription" section, with the same plan choice and confirmation.

The payment is deducted from your balance. If you buy a plan while your current subscription is still active, the days are **added to your current term**. Subscription purchases are non-refundable.

## When your subscription ends {#expiry}

Before your subscription ends, you get two reminders: **3 days** and **24 hours** in advance.

When the subscription expires, wholesale and team order slots that **haven't started yet** are frozen: uploading, publishing and taking them into work all stop. Slots that an executor has already started are fulfilled as usual.

With frozen slots, you can:

- **renew your subscription** — the slots unfreeze automatically;
- **cancel them** with a refund — see [Frozen slots](cancel-refunds.md#frozen).

If you do neither within **6 hours** of your subscription ending, the platform cancels the frozen slots itself and refunds the money to your balance.

Freezing doesn't apply to:

- **standard orders** — they don't depend on a subscription;
- **re-verifications** — they're never frozen, even if the original order was a wholesale or team order.
